# Recovery thresholds and decision rules

The athlete wrote these. Apply them as written. When a personal baseline is needed, use `context/profile.md`.

| Signal                         | Green = full session                                              | Yellow = keep intensity, cut hard volume ~20–30%                             | Orange = no hard work; easy only/rest                                                        |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sleep**                      | **≥6 hr** and you feel normal                                     | **5–6 hr** plus noticeable fatigue, or 2 short nights in a row               | **<5 hr**, or repeated poor sleep with clear fatigue                                         |
| **Muscle soreness**            | **0–2/10**, diffuse, normal walking/stairs, improves with warm-up | **2–3/10** and still noticeable after warm-up                                | **≥3/10**, worsening, or affecting mechanics                                                 |
| **Subjective fatigue**         | Normal or only mildly tired                                       | Clearly more tired than normal but functional                                | Heavy fatigue, unusually sluggish, or systemically "off"                                     |
| **Previous-day load**          | Roughly as planned, within ~1 RPE                                 | ~2 RPE harder than intended, or ~15–25% more hard work than planned          | Near-maximal/unplanned blow-up or substantially excessive duration/intensity                 |
| **HR at familiar easy output** | Within roughly **0–5 bpm** of normal                              | ~**5–8 bpm high** plus elevated RPE/poor feel                                | ~**8–10+ bpm high** with clearly abnormal effort/feel                                        |
| **RPE at familiar pace/power** | Normal or **+1**                                                  | Around **+2**                                                                | **+3 or more**                                                                               |
| **Warm-up response**           | Normal or improves within **10–15 min**                           | Still distinctly heavy/off after 10–15 min                                   | Gets worse as warm-up continues                                                              |
| **Pain**                       | **0–1/10**, or stable mild 2/10 with normal mechanics             | Persistent ~2/10 or becoming more noticeable                                 | **≥3/10**, sharp pain, weakness, instability, altered stride/pedal stroke                    |
| **Illness**                    | None / trivial residual symptoms                                  | Mild above-the-neck symptoms with normal energy                              | Fever, body aches, chest symptoms, worsening cough, significant fatigue                      |
| **HRV vs personal baseline**   | Within roughly **±10%**                                           | **10–20% below baseline** = mild yellow; **20–25%+ below** = stronger yellow | **~30%+ below baseline for 2+ days**, but only orange if other recovery signals are also bad |
| **Resting HR**                 | Within roughly **0–5 bpm** of normal baseline                     | ~**5–8 bpm elevated** plus another warning sign                              | **~8–10+ bpm elevated** with fatigue/illness/poor warm-up                                    |

## Decision rules

Do the full planned workout when:
- everything is green, or
- there is just one mild yellow, especially if the warm-up normalizes it.

Keep intensity but cut hard volume ~20–30% when:
- there are 2 yellow signals, or
- one clearly stronger yellow signal is present.

Example: planned 6×3 min hard → 4–5×3 min, rather than turning the whole session easy.

Replace intensity with easy training when:
- there is 1 orange signal, or
- several yellows stack together and do not improve during the warm-up.

Stop or skip the relevant workout when:
- focal pain changes mechanics,
- pain is sharp/worsening,
- there is systemic illness,
- dizziness/weakness occurs,
- or the warm-up makes things progressively worse.

## Important exceptions

- 6–7 hours of sleep is not inherently a problem. One 6-hour night + normal energy + normal warm-up = generally full workout.
- Very mild soreness is also not a problem. Diffuse 1–2/10 DOMS that improves during warm-up should generally not change the session.
- HRV never independently cancels a workout. Treat it as corroborating evidence only:
  - HRV down 25%, feel great, slept well, warm-up normal → train normally.
  - HRV down 25%, slept 5.5 hr, noticeably tired, legs heavy → reduce hard volume.
  - HRV down 30% for multiple days + RHR elevated + fatigue + bad warm-up → easy day.
- Apple Watch HRV spot readings are noisy, especially partial-day values. Use trends.

## Priority order when signals conflict

Pain/mechanics > illness/systemic fatigue > warm-up response > previous-day overload/sleep > HR/RPE mismatch > soreness > RHR > HRV

Default toward completing useful training, but reduce load when multiple independent signs point in the same direction. Mild sleep loss, slight soreness, or one ugly HRV reading should not waste a good training day.

## What the morning report can and cannot see

From Apple Health the coach has sleep, resting HR, HRV, yesterday's load, and steps. It does not have soreness, subjective fatigue, pain, warm-up response, or illness unless the athlete reported them. Morning adjustments therefore rest on the objective signals plus the most recent feedback in the profile. When the objective signals are yellow and nothing else is known, say what would tip it and let the athlete's warm-up decide.
